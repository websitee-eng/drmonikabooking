Common Questions

Questions
patients
ask us.

Can't find your answer? Call us at +91 9111-835-465 or WhatsApp us directly.

Do I need a referral to see Dr. Monika? +
No referral is needed. You can book directly via WhatsApp, phone, or the form above. Walk-ins are also welcome during clinic hours.
What should I bring to my first appointment? +
Bring any previous prescriptions, test reports (audiometry, CT scans etc.), and a list of current medications. This helps Dr. Monika give the most accurate diagnosis.
Does the clinic treat children? +
Yes, Dr. Monika provides gentle and specialised ENT care for patients of all ages including infants and children. Conditions like adenoids, ear infections, and tongue tie are commonly treated.
Are the surgeries minimally invasive? +
Wherever clinically appropriate, Dr. Monika uses minimally invasive techniques including endoscopic sinus surgery, laser-assisted procedures, and microdebrider-assisted surgeries to ensure faster recovery and less discomfort.
What are the clinic hours? +
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. For urgent queries outside these hours, you can WhatsApp the clinic and we'll respond as soon as possible.
How can I get a hearing aid fitted? +
Book a hearing evaluation appointment. Dr. Monika will conduct audiometry tests, assess your hearing loss type and degree, and recommend the most suitable hearing aid with full fitting and follow-up support.
